## Known limitations

1. **Recorded phase state.** P0/S00 was implemented and validated while `RESEARCH_STATE.yaml` stayed `PLANNED`: no
   canonical `transition()` was ever run. The G0 approval is valid for the transitions (verified in a synthetic
   copy of the repository), so the researcher can record them — step 1 below.
2. **No valid way to record `APPROVED_FOR_NEXT_PHASE`.** The transition `GATE_REVIEW → APPROVED_FOR_NEXT_PHASE` is
   human-only, but the provenance check rejects any non-`PLANNED` state without a canonical `TRANSITION` record
   (verified: a hand edit yields `STATE_PROVENANCE_INVALID`). The working path is to open P1 directly in `PLANNED`
   (step 2). A control-plane change that lets a human-authored gate decision be recorded is a design decision
   for the researcher; it was not made by the AI.
3. The two NOT_VERIFIABLE_IN_P0 items can only be verified by the first real P1 run; they are proposed as P1 exit
   criteria.
4. Hardware, programming and runtime are `NOT_AVAILABLE` (no board). P1 programming/runtime smoke is therefore
   out of reach until hardware exists; P1 can cover HLS → Vivado → XSA → bitstream → Vitis/ELF.

Commit, then write your own approval `audit/gates/approvals/G1-P1-S01-001.yaml`. Its content is your decision; the
